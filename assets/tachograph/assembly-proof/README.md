# Assembly proof

[Front](front.webp) · [Back](back.webp)

This visual proof uses the approved card as its base, including the sample truck window, static insignias and sample driver data. All 27 rank slots were recomposited from the redesigned shield atlas. The sample shows 96,735 Real Miles, Horizon Wolf as current, and Centurion as next with 3,265 miles remaining. Future ranks are grayscale with a padlock. Both sides were checked at 550 px wide. These are static comparison renders, not output from the Discord bot or a test of live driver fields.
