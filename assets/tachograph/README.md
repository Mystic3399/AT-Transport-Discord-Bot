# A&T tachograph card artwork

The front and back follow the navy, chrome and cyan tachograph concept. The backgrounds are empty artwork layers. Driver names, account details, mileage, career stats and rank labels belong in the renderer.

| Side | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| Front | Company logo and card title across the top, truck portrait and chip at left, identity and linked accounts at centre/right. Put the previous, current and next rank at the bottom with Verified Driver, 40K Mileage Club and the next-rank progress. |
| Back | Career record, current and next rank, mileage achievements and Hall of Fame at left. Put the Path to Immortal grid at right, in ascending mileage order. |

Use the original [rank badge PNGs](../badges/) for both sides, with their aspect ratios intact. Use full colour for unlocked ranks, a highlight around the current rank, and a grayscale treatment plus a lock for future ranks. Do not bake eligibility or achievement state into the artwork. The Hall of Fame trophy is separate from the permanent achievement medallion.

The original insignia sheets remain in [profile assets](../profile/). This pack also includes extracted insignias so they can be positioned separately. The truck portraits are concept artwork and should be checked against exact fleet models and liveries before enabling assignments.
